A full-featured commerce platform covering the complete order lifecycle, from catalogue and inventory management through cart, checkout, and fulfilment. `TypeScript` `Next.js` `Node.js` `PostgreSQL`

**Point of Sale (POS) System**
A point-of-sale system built for speed and accuracy at the counter. It handles sales transactions, real-time inventory deduction, receipt generation, and end-of-day reconciliation, with a data model designed around transactional integrity and auditable sales history, and reporting that gives owners a clear view of revenue, stock movement, and staff activity.
`TypeScript` `Node.js` `SQL`

**CRM Platform**
An enterprise customer relationship platform that unifies communication and process in one system. I worked on call handling and recording, SMS/MMS messaging, and a workflow automation engine that lets teams define triggers and actions without engineering involvement, integrating third-party telephony and messaging services behind clean, resilient service boundaries.
`TypeScript` `Node.js` `Workflow Automation`

A learning management system purpose-built for coaching centres, structured around batches, students, and instructors. The platform pairs a modular NestJS backend, with clearly separated domains and role-based permissions for administrators, teachers, and learners, with a cross-platform Flutter mobile application served by the same API.
`NestJS` `Flutter` `REST API`
